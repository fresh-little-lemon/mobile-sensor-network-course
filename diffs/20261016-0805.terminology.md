# 第19份录音：独立术语与技术范围核查

核查日期：2026-10-18。以下是编辑者另列的技术范围提醒，不是讲者新增发言。正文已经依据原始转写和独立ASR核对；不得把这里的标准知识无标记地写进讲话。

- DSSS：direct-sequence spread spectrum，直接序列扩频。2.4 GHz传统802.15.4采用DSSS/O-QPSK；原文“DSB spectrum”待ASR对照后处理。[TI CC2520手册，§17](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/cc2520.pdf#page=63)
- 6字节与127字节：传统2.4 GHz格式的4字节前导、1字节SFD和1字节PHY头合计6字节；最大127字节是PSDU/MPDU，含MAC首部及FCS，不等同于127字节净应用载荷。保留讲者用词，独立标出层次。[TI，§18](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/cc2520.pdf#page=64)
- 192微秒：TI芯片给出RX/TX与TX/RX转换时间192微秒；不应把全部ACK前后间隔一概当成同一种“SIFS”。[TI，§5.3、§19–20](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/cc2520.pdf#page=11)
- CW、NB、BE：传统有时隙CSMA-CA初始化NB=0、CW=2；CW是连续空闲检测的计数，与Wi-Fi随机竞争窗口不同。退避整数范围是0至2^BE−1，不是2^(BE−1)。忙时重设CW、增加NB并封顶增加BE；无时隙模式不对齐共同边界。[Microchip MRF24J40，§3.9](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/39776C.pdf#page=103)
- “两个时隙”不可混同超帧的16个大时隙；CSMA-CA使用backoff period边界。课上简化表述应另注范围，而不静默改写。
- IFS在需要ACK时位于ACK之后；短帧SIFS至少12符号，较长帧LIFS至少40符号。讲者将转换、ACK延迟、IFS统称SIFS的说法需要保留并提醒。[Microchip，§3.10](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/39776C.pdf#page=106)
- EIFS：课堂“物理与MAC忙闲不一致”的记忆法不能当成完整规范定义。IEEE工作组2004年澄清案按PHY接收错误或MAC FCS错误描述触发条件。[IEEE会议记录，802.11-04/1421r0](https://grouper.ieee.org/groups/802/11/Minutes/Cons_Minutes_Nov-2004.pdf)

## 补充范围提醒

- 停止等待（stop-and-wait）：全课按用户指定中文统一为“停止等待”，不使用“停等”。停止等待ARQ、退避、重传次数与信道接入失败次数是不同概念。
- Wi-Fi中的10与20微秒不能不加PHY限定：IEEE 802.11-2012的DS PHY表16-2列SIFS为10微秒、slot time为20微秒。正文保留讲者“10，抱歉20”的原自纠，不静默改值。不同PHY的参数可不同。[IEEE原标准镜像，§16.3.3](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf)
- PIFS与DCF：PIFS用于点协调优先接入等指定情形；普通DCF的数据接入不能与PCF启动无竞争期的信标行为混为一谈。正文保留讲者的原例子，不能因课堂把PIFS例子归于DCF，就认为这是一条普遍DCF信标规则。[IEEE原标准镜像，§9.3.2.3.4、§9.4](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf)
- RTS/CTS：本节讲者说传感器接入也“可能”使用RTS/CTS，两种转写一致；正文保留。本文核对的Microchip传统802.15.4 CSMA-CA流程是退避—CCA—数据发送，没有据此确认存在该课堂所称的可选RTS/CTS交换。扩展方案可另外研究，不能只凭讲者这句话写成基础标准功能。

## Technical checks

- EIFS is triggered by an erroneous received frame, not every PHY/NAV disagreement or mere noise. The lecture's “or vice versa” is overbroad. [IEEE official 2004 clarification, PDF p.82](https://grouper.ieee.org/groups/802/11/Minutes/Cons_Minutes_Nov-2004.pdf#page=82)
- Ending EIFS requires an error-free received frame; reading only a header is insufficient. Resume applicable DIFS/backoff, not immediate transmission. [IEEE 802.11-2012 §9.3.2.3.7, printed p.828](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf#page=926)
- Either physical or virtual sensing can indicate busy. NAV updates have validity, address and greater-value conditions; they are not unconditional assignments from every header. [§9.3.2.1 and §9.3.2.4](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf#page=926)
- ACK Duration can be zero; fragmented exchanges provide exceptions. [§9.3.2.5, printed p.830](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf#page=928)
- Backoff freezes on busy and resumes after applicable DIFS/EIFS. Post-backoff can be required without another queued transmission. [§9.3.4.3, printed p.839](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf#page=937)
- For individually addressed frames, the threshold comparison uses PSDU length, strictly greater. Protection/NAV-distribution exceptions exist. Hidden nodes are not themselves the mandatory threshold test. [§9.3.5, printed p.842](https://mrncciew.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/ieee-802-11-2012.pdf#page=940)
